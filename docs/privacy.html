01. Summary & Core Promise

Cockroach Community is an open-source, peer-to-peer (P2P) decentralized mesh communication application for Android. It enables communication, chatting, file transfers, and voice/video calling directly between devices without requiring an active internet connection, cellular reception, or centralized servers.

🛡️ Our Promise: Your data belongs exclusively to your device. We do not maintain any central servers or databases, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not track or sell any information.

02. Zero Cloud Data Collection

Because Cockroach Community runs on direct device-to-device wireless mesh networking:

  • No User Accounts on Cloud Servers: You do not need to register with an email address, phone number, or password on any remote server.
  • No Cloud Message Storage: Messages, files, and call sessions travel directly between devices via Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth. They are never transmitted to or through a remote central server.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application contains no tracking software development kits (SDKs), no crash-reporting telemetry services, and no advertising trackers.

03. Android Permissions & Justification

Cockroach Community requests only the minimal permissions required to establish offline local peer-to-peer links and deliver core communications. Here is an exact breakdown:

Bluetooth & Nearby Devices BLUETOOTH_SCAN, ADVERTISE, CONNECT

Required to discover nearby peer devices and establish offline Bluetooth/Nearby Connections mesh links. No Bluetooth data is transmitted outside your local communication zone.

Wi-Fi Direct & Local Network NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE

Used to initiate high-speed Wi-Fi Direct peer-to-peer file transfers and direct local sockets without needing an external Wi-Fi router or internet access.

Location (Framework Requirement) ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Android OS requires location permission to perform Wi-Fi and Bluetooth beacon scans. The app does not read, log, store, or share your GPS coordinates or physical location.

Camera & Microphone CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO

Used exclusively when you place or accept a direct local peer-to-peer voice/video call or capture photos to send to peers. Audio and video streams are routed directly to the connected peer over local WebRTC and are never recorded without your command.

Notifications & Foreground Service POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Allows the app to maintain background mesh synchronization and alert you when an incoming call or message arrives from a nearby peer.

04. Cryptography & End-to-End Security

Security is built directly into the local architecture:

  • Hardware-Backed Android KeyStore: Cryptographic keys (Elliptic Curve Key Agreement / ECDH) are generated directly inside your device's secure hardware element. Private keys cannot be extracted.
  • Local AES-GCM Encryption: Payloads exchanged between paired peers are encrypted with derived session secrets to prevent interception over local radio frequencies.
  • Decentralized Identities: Peer identities are cryptographically verified through device-generated public keys, eliminating reliance on certificate authorities or third-party identity servers.

05. Local Storage & Data Deletion

All your conversations, contacts, transferred files, and settings are stored locally on your device's internal storage (via Room Database and DataStore).

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete individual conversations, messages, or files inside the app at any time.
  • You can immediately wipe 100% of the application's data by going to Android Settings → Apps → Cockroach Community → Storage → Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.

06. Third-Party Services & Trackers

Cockroach Community does NOT integrate:

  • No advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.)
  • No third-party analytics libraries (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment)
  • No crash-reporting trackers sending stack traces to third parties

07. Children's Privacy

Cockroach Community does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or sent over the internet, the application complies fully with COPPA and global privacy regulations.
